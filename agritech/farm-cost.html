Description

Track your expenses for the season to calculate your break-even point and ensure your farm is profitable
This page breaks down the estimated cost for planting 2.5 acres of beans, including seeds, fertilizer and labor.

Tools Needed

  • A notebook or spreadsheet (or this web page!)
  • Receipts from your local agrovet
  • Labor records

Season Expenses

Item Quantity Estimated Cost
Certified Beans Seeds 50 kg 15,000
DAP Fertilizer 2 Bags 14,000
Labor(Land prep & planting) 4 Days 8,000
Fungicide(El nino prep) 2 litres 4,500
Total 41,500

How to use the tracker

  1. Gather all receipts from your seed and fertilizer purchases
  2. Log every single cost, no matter how small (transport, labor, chemicals)
  3. Update the "Actual Cost" column at the end of the season
  4. Compare your total cost against your harvest revenue to see your profit
